Privacy Policy

Hannahs eBay Lister (Listing Writer) is a personal tool for creating eBay listings from item photos.

Photos and drafts

Photos and listing drafts are saved in your browser. When you request AI processing, resized photos and listing information are sent through the app's server to Anthropic to sort items and generate listing text. This instance does not maintain a server database of photos or drafts. Its optional cloud background-drafting feature is not enabled.

Your eBay connection

If you connect eBay, the app stores your refresh token in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. The server uses it when needed to communicate with eBay. Short-lived access tokens may be held temporarily in server memory. The app does not receive your eBay password.

Services and sharing

Vercel hosts the app. Anthropic processes information submitted for AI generation. When you choose to publish, photos and listing details are sent to eBay. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. This app does not sell your data or maintain a database of buyer or order records.

Your control

You can disconnect eBay in the app to remove its connection cookie. Clearing cookies removes the saved connection; clearing the app's site storage removes locally saved photos and drafts. These actions do not delete listings already published on eBay.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the account owner through the policy repository. Do not include private information in public issues.
